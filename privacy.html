Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Purpose and data access

Lab Drive (rclone) is a personal integration operated by Jincheng Gao. With the account owner’s authorization, rclone can list, read, upload, modify and delete Google Drive files. File contents and metadata are accessed to mount the Drive as a directory and transfer the files selected by the owner.

Storage and handling

Files remain in the owner’s Google Drive unless the owner copies, modifies or deletes them. Copies and temporary caches may be stored on the owner’s research server or computer. OAuth credentials are stored in access-restricted configuration files on those devices and are used to authenticate with Google.

Sharing and use

The integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties for those purposes. Transfers are between Google Drive and the owner’s configured devices. Google user data accessed by this integration is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access in Google Account security settings at any time. Revocation prevents further authorized access; it does not automatically delete previously downloaded files or caches. Those can be removed directly from the configured devices. Cloud files can be managed or deleted in Google Drive.

Google API policy

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For questions, contact the operator via this project’s issue tracker. Do not post private files or credentials.
